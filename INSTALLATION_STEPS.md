# 📱 MARSHALL APP - INSTALLATION STEPS

## Copy & Paste Instructions

Follow these steps exactly. Just copy and paste when needed.

---

## STEP 1: DOWNLOAD ANDROID STUDIO

**Time: 5-10 minutes**

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, etc.)
2. Go to this website:
   ```
   https://developer.android.com/studio
   ```
3. Look for the big blue button that says **"Download Android Studio"**
4. Click it
5. Choose your computer type (Windows, Mac, or Linux)
6. Wait for the download to finish
7. Double-click the downloaded file
8. Follow the setup wizard - just click "Next" and "Finish"
9. Open Android Studio when it's done

**✅ DONE WITH STEP 1**

---

## STEP 2: CLONE THE MARSHALL PROJECT

**Time: 5 minutes**

### In Android Studio:

1. Click **File** (top left corner)
2. Hover over **New**
3. Click **Project from Version Control**
4. A new window opens
5. Make sure **Git** is selected on the left
6. In the **URL** field, paste this:
   ```
   https://github.com/medina3580health-cmd/Marshall.git
   ```
7. The **Directory** will auto-fill - that's fine
8. Click the blue **Clone** button
9. **WAIT 3-5 MINUTES**
   - You'll see "Syncing..." at the bottom
   - This is normal, just wait
10. When it says "Sync finished", you're done

**✅ DONE WITH STEP 2**

---

## STEP 3: ENABLE DEVELOPER MODE ON YOUR PHONE

**Time: 2 minutes**

### On your Android Phone:

1. Open **Settings** (gear icon)
2. Scroll down to the **very bottom**
3. Look for **"About Phone"** or **"About Device"**
4. Tap it
5. Scroll down to find **"Build Number"**
6. **TAP "BUILD NUMBER" 7 TIMES QUICKLY**
   - After 7 taps, you'll see a message: "You are now a developer!"
7. Go **back** (press back button)
8. Now you should see **"Developer Options"** - tap it
9. Look for **"USB Debugging"**
10. Toggle it **ON** (it turns blue/green)
11. A popup asks "Allow USB Debugging?" - tap **ALLOW**

**✅ DONE WITH STEP 3**

---

## STEP 4: CONNECT PHONE TO COMPUTER

**Time: 1 minute**

1. Take your USB cable
2. Plug one end into your phone (bottom of phone usually)
3. Plug the other end into your computer
4. **UNLOCK YOUR PHONE**
5. A popup might appear on your phone asking about USB - tap **ALLOW**

**✅ DONE WITH STEP 4**

---

## STEP 5: RUN THE APP

**Time: 2 minutes**

### In Android Studio:

1. Look at the top of the window
2. Find the green **▶️ PLAY BUTTON** (Run button)
3. Click it
4. A window appears: **"Select Deployment Target"**
5. You should see your phone listed (it might say your phone name or model)
6. **Click on your phone** to select it
7. Click the blue **OK** button
8. **WAIT 30 SECONDS - 1 MINUTE**
   - You'll see messages at the bottom
   - This is the app building and installing
9. Look at your phone - you should see:
   - "Building..." message
   - Then "App installed successfully"
10. The app will open automatically!

**✅ DONE WITH STEP 5 - APP IS INSTALLED!**

---

## STEP 6: USE THE APP

**Your phone should now show the Marshall app!**

### Here's how to use it:

1. You'll see **"🐱 Cat Medical Records"** at the top
2. It says **"Files: 0"** because it's empty
3. Click the **blue + button** at the bottom right
4. A file picker appears with sample files
5. Click on files to select them (they get a checkmark ✓)
6. Choose these example files:
   - vaccination_record_2024.pdf
   - cat_photo_1.jpg
   - vet_checkup_jan2024.pdf
7. Click **"Add Files"** button
8. **DONE!** Your files appear in the folder
9. To remove a file, click the **X** button next to it

**✅ SUCCESS! APP IS WORKING!**

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Problem: "Gradle sync failed"
**Solution:**
- Click **File** → **Sync Now**
- Wait 2 minutes
- If still fails, close and reopen Android Studio

### Problem: "Device not recognized"
**Solution:**
- Check USB cable is plugged into both phone and computer
- Make sure phone is **unlocked**
- Try a different USB port
- Restart your phone
- On phone: Go to Settings → Developer Options → Check "USB Debugging" is ON

### Problem: "Can't find my phone in deployment target"
**Solution:**
- Unplug USB cable
- Wait 5 seconds
- Plug it back in
- A popup on phone might appear - tap ALLOW
- Try running again

### Problem: "App won't open"
**Solution:**
- Look at bottom of Android Studio - see the error message
- Take a screenshot and check if USB Debugging is enabled
- Restart Android Studio completely
- Try again
